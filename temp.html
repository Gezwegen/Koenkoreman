Stories From The Empire

Who am I?

People do not call me. I simply appear. When official paperwork insists on a name, it says Koen Koreman.

At Howest I lecture, debug, and occasionally test whether a room still remembers what a variable is. This site is a lovingly unserious record of why that job requires both knowledge and emotional resilience.

Teenagers

I love my children especially when they wake up at 6 AM for cartoons and demand breakfast like tiny project managers and I love my students except the ones who are younger than 18.

I once explained that 1 + 1 = 10. That is correct in binary, but most of the time people are confused. It makes them wonder what, exactly, I am teaching my children at home.

My students are dear to me too, even the brave souls who do not always attend. Their absence gives me time to craft 222-IQ hints: technically leading to the answer, yet emotionally encrypted. The Log4j period was a special work of art..

Laughter Prolongs Life

During a Forensic Analysis lecture, I was talking about Windows. Not my favorite window to open, but education demands sacrifice.

Suddenly, a very loud laugh burst from a student's phone. The whole class folded, including me. Checking your phone during lectures is dangerous; comedy has timing.

Did it improve the mood? Absolutely. Before that, the faces in the room were quietly asking, "Why am I here?". But still: do not do it.
