# Koen Koremans: King of HOWEST - Meme Site Info

Here is a curated collection of lore, quotes, and facts about Koen Koremans (also known as **KoenKje** or **KoenK**) based on the famous "KoenK Empire" and his life as a lecturer at Howest. This information is perfect for building a fun, harmless meme/prank site dedicated to him!

## 👑 The Legend Himself
*   **Titles:** King of Howest, Ruler of the KoenK Empire, Master of Unix, and Reluctant Debugger of Windows.
*   **His Domain:** Lectures OS (Operating Systems), Programming Fundamentals, and Forensic Analysis at Howest.
*   **Natural Habitat:** Linux. (macOS is "technically allowed in the Unix-ish neighborhood").
*   **Unnatural Habitat:** Windows. ("Not my favorite window to open, but education demands sacrifice").

## 🗣️ Catchphrases & "Koenisms"
*   **"1 + 1 = 10"** - Certified binary breakfast wisdom. (He once explained this to his kids, leaving everyone confused about what he teaches them at home).
*   **"222 IQ Hints"** - His trademark way of helping students: hints that technically lead to the answer, yet are "emotionally encrypted." (The Log4j period was apparently a special work of art).
*   **"Just raise your head. The code is on the TV."** - The ultimate exam day frustration when students spend 30 minutes searching for code that is glowing right in front of them.

## 😤 Royal Grievances (Things that break his soul)
*   **Variables:** When students ask what a variable is after months of OS and Programming Fundamentals courses. (It does "something tender and dramatic to his soul 😭").
*   **Teenagers:** He loves his students, *except the ones who are younger than 18*.
*   **Colleague's Terminal Setups:** Customizing terminals is powerful, but sometimes colleagues take it too far, leaving him utterly speechless.
*   **Mobile Phones in Class:** During a Forensic Analysis lecture, a loud laugh burst from a student's phone and folded the whole class. (He admits it improved the mood, but still... "do not do it").

## 🎮 The National Sport of the Empire
